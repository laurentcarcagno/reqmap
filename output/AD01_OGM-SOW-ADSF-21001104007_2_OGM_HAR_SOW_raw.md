## **1 Introduction** 

This Contractor’s Statement of Work (SOW) defines work to be performed by the Contractor under the contractual authority of the OGM Customer Contractor, Airbus Defence and Space. 

The OGM Implementation Phase will be conducted in three major phases: 

- Phase B2: Preliminary Definition 

- Phase C/D: Detailed Definition and Qualification/Acceptance 

- Phase E: Utilisation 

- Phase F: Disposal 

This Statement of Work (SOW) is applicable in conjunction with the _Generic SOW (GSOW) [AD-02],_ which defines the equipment generic requirements. In case of conflict, this specific statement of work shall have precedence. 

## **1.1 Scope of the Document** 

The equipment subject of this Statement of Work (SOW) is the DC harness (hereinafter referred to only as “harness”) for the OGM Project, which is defined by the Harness Requirements Specification [AD-03]. This Harness SOW defines the tasks of the harness supplier for: 

- The design, development, qualification, manufacturing, integration, test and delivery of the equipment, 

- The provision of tooling, test and support equipment, support services and transportation services to support instrument and spacecraft assembly, integration, test and launch, associated with the harness operation and handling. 

- The preparation and delivery of documentation. 

In addition, tasks and deliverables are categorized into baseline tasks and optional tasks. Both kinds of tasks may occur in this SOW. Unless not specifically identified to the contrary, all task and deliverables specified hereafter shall belong to the baseline tasks. Options are collected at §4.4 Options. 

The requirements of this SSOW shall also be made applicable to the contractually specified Lower Level Subcontractors/Suppliers, as pertinent to their work shares. Therefore the word "Supplier" shall be interpreted as “Supplier and Lower Level Subcontractors/Suppliers”, if not specifically restricted. Hardware from other sources, such as OTS items, shall be procured according to the equivalent technical, quality and safety related requirements.

## **4.3.1 Design** 

HAR SSOW-7 / Electrical detail design

_The Prime/Customer shall provide the Supplier with a harness EICD containing:_ 

- _Signal types, defining all type of interfaces and cable used._ 

- _Unit list, units with interfaces to be connected_ 

- _Harness connector list, defined all connector types as generic P/N, eg. DAMA 15S._ 

- _Signal list, containing all signals wired in the EICD_ 

- _Pin2Pin list, which defines the E2E connectivity between pins, including dismountabilities and identification of the main/redundant lines. In the Pin2Pin cable types are defined as generic P/N, unless Proprietary I/Fs, if any, which are defined with complete P/N:_ 

   - _For non-dissipative lines and lines not subject of Vd and resistance analysis, the gauge is also defined in the Pin2Pin, eg. TP24_ 

   - _Dissipative lines and lines subject of Vd and resistance analysis, the gauge is either not defined, eg. TPxx, or provisionally defined but to be confirmed with the analysis._ 

_The Supplier shall then be responsible for the electrical detail design of the harness for manufacturing and integration._ 

_The electrical detail design shall consist on:_ 

- _Harness connector list, with P/N selection for all connectors including backshells, screwlocks screengrills and any other accessory._ 

- _Wiring Diagrams, introducing all necessary modifications for manufacturing and integration of the harness, eg. all splices, whilst fulfilling the electrical connectivity defined by the Prime/Customer._ 

- _Wiring list (aka. Manufacturing LinkList) comprising the manufacturing configuration of the harness, with P/N’s and all modifications introduced in the schematics for manufacturing and integration of the harness._ 

   - _P/N selection of all cable types and unique identification for each cable in the harness._
